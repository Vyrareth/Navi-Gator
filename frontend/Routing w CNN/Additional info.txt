Additional info:

To run saving the route, before doing so, you must update the environmental variables CLOUDFLARE_R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID and CLOUDFLARE_R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY respectively on the terminal fit for environmental variables

Order of running the program:
assure all files are in the same folder location on your device
make sure the html is inside of a folder called "template"
run 2ndtest_routing_app_updated - Copy.py, a server number of 0.0.0.0:0000 format should appear on the output screen, click on it to have a local connection
click on 2 different destinations then click on "Plan Trip", a route should show from the 2 different points and segments w lats and longs 
click on "Save Route" to utilize the CNN and output a new route based on rating and after it does its calculations, a new route should show
